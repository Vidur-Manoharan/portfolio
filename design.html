    Precision Engineering Through Part Design

              Built through intensive practice in SOLIDWORKS, this collection represents my journey in mechanical part modeling, engineering design, and CAD mastery. Each model was created as part of my structured preparation for the Certified SOLIDWORKS Associate (CSWA) and Certified SOLIDWORKS Professional (CSWP) certification paths, focusing on precision, design intent, and manufacturable geometry.
    What began as certification preparation evolved into a deeper understanding of parametric modeling, engineering analysis, and real-world design problem solving.

    Throughout this journey, I developed hands-on experience in:

    • Parametric part modeling
    • Sketch-driven design methodology
    • Feature-based solid modeling
    • Design intent and model optimization
    • Engineering dimensioning and geometric accuracy
    • Mechanical component development

    Having successfully completed both CSWA and CSWP certifications, this collection represents an important milestone in my engineering journey.

    Bringing Ideas Into Reality with 3D Printing



              Powered by the precision of my Bambu Lab A1 Combo, my design workflow combines creativity, engineering, and rapid prototyping to transform digital concepts into functional physical products. Using Autodesk Fusion and SOLIDWORKS, I design and develop models that blend aesthetics, practicality, and manufacturability.

    What began as a passion for 3D printing evolved into a hands-on engineering journey focused on:

    • Rapid prototyping & iterative design
    • Design for Manufacturing (DFM)
    • Functional product development
    • Mechanical and architectural modeling
    • Creative problem-solving through additive manufacturing


    From architectural models and utility tools to personalised creations, these designs represent my ongoing journey in 3D printing, product development, and hands-on innovation.

    Explore the collection below to see how design, engineering, and creativity come together through additive manufacturing.

    • BMW MPower

      Inspired by iconic motorsport heritage, this sculptural display piece captures the bold energy of performance and precision. Featuring a clean geometric form with signature racing colors, it transforms automotive passion into a modern desk accessory—perfect for enthusiasts who appreciate design, speed, and craftsmanship.
